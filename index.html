Current role

Motional — Senior Engineering Manager, Autonomous Systems

Release management & performance evaluation · 2020–present

I lead a cross-functional engineering team responsible for release management and performance evaluation of Motional’s autonomous driving software—ensuring safe, scalable, and high-quality deployments across the autonomy stack.

Release strategy & execution

I define and drive the release strategy for the autonomy platform—structured validation frameworks so every software release meets strict safety, performance, and reliability standards. That includes release criteria, alignment with cross-functional stakeholders, and data-driven go/no-go decisions. My team de-risks deployments and delivers consistent, high-quality software for internal milestones and external demonstrations.

Performance evaluation & metrics strategy

I lead a metrics-driven evaluation system for autonomy performance across safety, comfort, and decision-making. By scaling analysis across large volumes of real-world and simulated data, we surface gaps, track improvements over time, and give engineering and product teams actionable insight.

Technical direction & roadmap alignment

Through structured analysis and cross-team collaboration, I help shape the technical roadmap—calling out systemic issues, prioritizing improvements, and aligning engineering with business and safety goals. My team is a central signal for system performance and informed decisions across autonomy, infrastructure, and product.

Organizational impact

  • Improved release confidence via scalable validation and evaluation
  • Enabled faster, data-driven decisions across engineering
  • Reduced regression risk through structured performance tracking and analysis
