About

My work sits at the intersection of autonomous systems, sensor and platform validation, and cross-functional leadership. I care about clear ownership, fast feedback from real-world operation, and structured approaches to risk—RCA, FTA, and FMEA—when systems behave in ways we need to understand and improve.

Background spans automotive safety and restraint systems through ADAS/AV sensors and large-scale software test organizations. I’m motivated by building teams that scale with the product and by making complex technical tradeoffs understandable to partners across engineering.
